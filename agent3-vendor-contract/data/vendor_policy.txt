ENTERPRISE VENDOR & PROCUREMENT POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Approved by: Chief Procurement Officer

========================================
SECTION 1: VENDOR ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
========================================

1.1 All vendors must be registered and approved by the Procurement Department before any purchase order is issued.

1.2 Vendor Eligibility Requirements:
- Valid business registration certificate
- Minimum 3 years of operational history
- Annual revenue of at least $1 million USD
- No active litigation or regulatory sanctions
- ISO 9001 or equivalent quality certification for hardware vendors
- SOC 2 Type II compliance for all SaaS and cloud vendors
- GDPR compliance certification for vendors handling EU data

1.3 Blacklisted Vendors: Vendors with unresolved disputes, fraud history, or two consecutive SLA breaches are placed on the blacklist and are ineligible for new contracts.

1.4 Preferred Vendor Status: Vendors with 3+ years of satisfactory performance, zero SLA breaches, and competitive pricing may be granted Preferred Vendor status, entitling them to expedited approval and priority consideration.

========================================
SECTION 2: CONTRACT COMPLIANCE RULES
========================================

2.1 All contracts must be reviewed and signed by the Legal Department before execution.

2.2 Contracts exceeding $100,000 USD require approval from the CFO and CEO.

2.3 Contracts exceeding $500,000 USD require Board of Directors approval.

2.4 Contract Duration:
- Standard contracts: 1-3 years
- Cloud and SaaS contracts: 1-2 years with annual review
- Hardware contracts: up to 5 years

2.5 Auto-renewal clauses must be flagged 90 days before renewal date for review.

2.6 All contracts must include:
- Clear SLA definitions with measurable KPIs
- Penalty clauses for SLA breaches
- Data security and confidentiality clauses
- Termination for convenience clause (30-day notice)
- Dispute resolution mechanism

2.7 Contracts with foreign vendors must comply with applicable export control laws and require additional legal review.

========================================
SECTION 3: SLA REQUIREMENTS
========================================

3.1 Minimum SLA Standards by Category:

Cloud Services:
- Uptime: 99.9% minimum (99.99% preferred)
- Incident response: 4 hours for critical, 8 hours for high priority
- Data backup: Daily with 30-day retention
- Security patches: Within 24 hours of critical CVE disclosure

Software/SaaS:
- Uptime: 99.9% minimum
- Support response: 24 hours standard, 4 hours for critical
- Data export capability required

Hardware:
- Replacement SLA: 48 hours for critical equipment
- On-site support: Next business day
- Warranty: Minimum 3 years

Logistics:
- On-time delivery: 98% minimum
- Damage rate: Less than 0.5%
- Tracking: Real-time visibility required

3.2 SLA Breach Penalties:
- First breach: Written warning + service credit
- Second breach: 10% contract value penalty
- Third breach: Contract termination rights activated

3.3 SLA reporting must be submitted monthly by the vendor.

========================================
SECTION 4: PAYMENT TERMS
========================================

4.1 Standard Payment Terms:
- Net-30: Standard for software and services
- Net-45: Standard for consulting and professional services
- Net-60: Standard for hardware and large equipment
- Net-15: For logistics and time-sensitive services

4.2 Early Payment Discounts:
- 2/10 Net-30: 2% discount if paid within 10 days
- 1/15 Net-45: 1% discount if paid within 15 days

4.3 Late Payment Penalties:
- 1.5% per month on overdue balances
- Vendors may suspend services after 60 days of non-payment

4.4 Purchase Orders above $10,000 require three competitive quotes.
Purchase Orders above $50,000 require formal RFP process.
Purchase Orders above $200,000 require competitive bidding with minimum 3 vendors.

4.5 Advance payments are not permitted without CFO approval.

4.6 All payments must be processed through the approved ERP system (Oracle).

========================================
SECTION 5: DATA SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
========================================

5.1 All vendors with access to company data must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

5.2 Cloud vendors must provide:
- SOC 2 Type II report annually
- Penetration test results annually
- Data residency confirmation (data must remain in approved regions)

5.3 Vendors must notify the company within 24 hours of any data breach.

5.4 Annual vendor security assessments are mandatory for contracts exceeding $50,000.

5.5 Vendors must comply with:
- GDPR (for EU data)
- CCPA (for California resident data)
- ISO 27001 (preferred for all technology vendors)

========================================
SECTION 6: VENDOR PERFORMANCE REVIEW
========================================

6.1 Quarterly performance reviews are conducted for all active contracts above $25,000.

6.2 Annual reviews are conducted for all active contracts.

6.3 Performance Scorecard (100 points):
- SLA compliance: 40 points
- Quality of deliverables: 25 points
- Responsiveness and communication: 20 points
- Pricing competitiveness: 15 points

6.4 Vendors scoring below 60 are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Vendors scoring below 40 are subject to contract termination.

6.5 Vendors scoring above 85 for two consecutive years qualify for Preferred Vendor status.

========================================
SECTION 7: CONTRACT RENEWAL & TERMINATION
========================================

7.1 Renewal Process:
- Procurement team initiates review 90 days before expiry
- Legal review required for any modified terms
- CFO approval required for contracts above $100,000

7.2 Termination for Cause:
- Immediate termination for fraud, data breach, or criminal activity
- 30-day notice for repeated SLA breaches (3 or more)
- 60-day notice for performance issues

7.3 Termination for Convenience:
- 30-day written notice required
- No penalty if notice period is honored
- Vendor must provide transition assistance for 60 days post-termination

7.4 Upon termination, vendors must:
- Return all company data within 30 days
- Provide data in portable format
- Certify data deletion in writing
